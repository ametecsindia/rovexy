ROVEXY is a work app for collection agencies and field teams. Your company signs you in; the app works only with your company's ROVEXY workspace.

For field staff
• See your assigned cases with dues, address and history.
• Call customers from your own phone; your phone's recorder keeps the call on record.
• Save the outcome: promise to pay, payment collected (with slip photo), callback, not reachable and more.
• Field map: customers near you, routes and one-tap navigation.
• Visits are verified at the customer's address.
• Chat and internet voice calls with your supervisor.

For supervisors and managers (web console)
• Live team status and location during work hours.
• Call recordings, outcomes and payments in one place.
• Allocate and transfer cases, verify payment slips.

Transparent by design
• A ROVEXY notification is always shown while location, a call or a session is active.
• Screen and camera sessions start only after you accept them, every time.
• "What my company can see" shows exactly what is collected.

ROVEXY needs an account from your employer. Companies can request a demo at https://rovexy.app.
